Horizon 2020 Traction Project

In this project, I pioneered the development of next-generation adaptation strategies for real-time multimedia delivery, specifically designed to facilitate synchronized, global opera co-creation. Beyond the performance itself, I particpated in the development of an immersive experience engine that provides a sophisticated virtual environment, allowing audiences to socialize and engage both before and after the curtain rises.

Completed

Real-time Secure Resource Leasing in 5G

For this project, I designed and implemented a pioneering blockchain-based distributed solution to enable seamless, end-to-end service provisioning across multi-provider environments. After successfully validating the architecture through a concrete, real-world use case, the system was adopted as a core prototype for future Huawei product lines.

Completed

Horizon 2020 Newton Project

In this project, I pioneered an innovative IoT-based solution designed to bridge the digital divide by enabling seamless remote access to Fab Labs. I subsequently directed the Fab Lab STEM pilot deployment at St. Patrick’s B.N.S. in Dublin, successfully validating the technology's real-world impact and scalability in an educational setting.
