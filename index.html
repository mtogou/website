Biography

I received my BSc in Computer Science and MSc in Computer Networks from Al Akhawayn University in Ifrane, Morocco, and my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Montreal, Canada and the University of Technology of Troyes, France.

My expertise was forged in the demanding field of Intelligent Transportation Systems, where my doctoral thesis pioneered Quality of Service (QoS) enhancements for time-sensitive infotainment applications in the context of connected vehicles.

As a postdoctoral researcher, I expanded my horizons into the study of scalability and resiliency in cloud computing, along with blockchain-enabled solutions for next-generation networks (5G/6G). I have published more than 50 peer-reviewed scientific articles in top journals and flagship conferences, and I continue to serve as a member of the technical program committee for several international conferences.
